Relevance of Projects in VIT

Explore the importance of various project methodologies taught in our curriculum.

  • SDP
  • EDI

  • Course Projects

Software Development Project (SDP)

SDP focuses on the core principles of software engineering. Students learn to gather requirements, design system architecture, write clean and maintainable code, and properly test their applications before deployment.

  • Simulates real-world software lifecycles.
  • Focuses on teamwork and version control.

Engineering Design and Innovation (EDI)

EDI challenges students to think outside the box. It bridges the gap between hardware, software, and mechanical principles. Students prototype and build innovative solutions using microcontrollers, sensors, and basic circuits.

  • Encourages multidisciplinary learning.
  • Hands-on prototyping and problem-solving.

Design Thinking (DT)

Design Thinking is a human-centered approach to innovation. It integrates the needs of people, the possibilities of technology, and the requirements for business success. It helps students emphasize empathy, ideation, and rapid prototyping.

  • Focuses heavily on user empathy.
  • Iterative process of testing and refinement.

Course Projects

Course projects are tied to specific subjects within the semester (like Database Management, Web Technologies, etc.). They ensure that the theoretical knowledge learned in lectures is properly applied through practical implementation.

  • Deepens subject-specific knowledge.
  • Allows for continuous assessment of practical skills.
